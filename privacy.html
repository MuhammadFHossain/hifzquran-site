Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

The short version. Hifz Quran asks for no permissions at all. No microphone, no speech recognition, no camera, no contacts, no location, no notifications. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no ads, and we run no servers of our own. Your progress lives on your iPhone and goes nowhere else. We never see it. Under Apple’s definitions this app is “Data Not Collected.”

This policy explains what Hifz Quran (“the app”, “we”, “us”) does with your information. Hifz Quran is made by Farhan Hossain. If anything here is unclear, email contact@farhanhossain.com.

What changed in this version. The earlier version of this policy described a “recite from memory” feature that used the microphone and speech recognition. That feature has been removed from the app in full. There is no microphone access, no speech recognition, and no speech model in the app. Testing your memory is now done by hiding the āyāt on the page and revealing them yourself. The app requests no permissions of any kind. iCloud sync has also been dropped: your progress is kept on your iPhone and is not copied anywhere.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the Hifz Quran app for iPhone and this website, hifzquran.org.

2. Information we collect

Personal information we collect: none. Hifz Quran has no login, no user accounts, and no backend of ours that stores your data. We do not run analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any tracking, and the app contains no third-party SDKs.

The app creates and stores the following on your device so it can work:

  • Your memorization progress. Which passages you are working on, what you have marked as memorized, and your review schedule.
  • Your test results. Whether a hide-and-reveal test came out solid or shaky, which is what decides when a passage comes back around for review.
  • Your settings. Small preferences like your chosen script, translation, reciter, and repeat counts.
  • Recitation audio you have played or downloaded, cached so it works offline.

This data stays in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it.

3. Permissions: none

Hifz Quran requests no permissions whatsoever. You will not see a permission prompt at any point, because there is nothing for the app to ask for. It does not use, and cannot use:

  • the microphone,
  • speech recognition, on your device or Apple’s,
  • the camera, photos, contacts, calendars, location, or notifications.

Your recitation is never heard, recorded, transcribed, or stored, by us or by anyone else. When you test yourself, you recite aloud and tap to reveal each āyah to check your own memory. The app plays audio and shows the page. It has no way to hear you.

The app does declare iOS’s background audio mode. That is ordinary playback, so a repeat drill keeps playing with your screen locked. It carries no access to anything on your phone.

4. Where your progress is kept

Everything the app stores stays on your iPhone, in the app’s own private storage. Hifz Quran does not sync, does not use iCloud, and does not copy your progress anywhere. There is no account attached to it and nothing for us to look at. Deleting the app deletes it, so use your iPhone’s own device backup if you want to keep it when you change phones.

5. Network access

The Quran text, the mushaf pages, the fonts, and all ten translations are bundled inside the app and need no network at all. The app uses the internet for one thing: fetching recitation audio when you play or download it, from two public Quran audio hosts.

  • everyayah.com, one MP3 per āyah, for nine of the reciters.
  • mp3quran.net, surah recordings, for Badr al-Turki.

These requests fetch public audio files. They carry no account, no identifier for you, and none of your progress, and neither host is ours. Audio is cached on your iPhone after the first play, so a drill you have played once works offline afterwards.

6. No advertising, analytics, or tracking

Hifz Quran contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking software of any kind. We do not build a profile of you and do not track you across other apps or websites. Under Apple’s definitions, Hifz Quran is “Data Not Collected,” and the App Store privacy label says exactly that.

7. Purchases

Hifz Quran is free and has no in-app purchases. If we ever added a paid feature, any purchase would be handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, and we would never see your card or billing details.

8. This website

This site is a simple set of static pages. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies and does not ask you to sign in. Our hosting provider may keep basic, non-identifying server logs (such as the type of request and a truncated address) to keep the site running and secure. If you email us, we keep your message so we can reply.

9. Children

Hifz Quran is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because the app has no accounts and collects no personal data, there is nothing for a child to submit to us. If you have a concern, email us and we will help.

10. Your choices and rights

Because your data lives on your device, you are in control of it. You can reset your progress in the app and clear the cached audio from Settings, and deleting the app removes its data from your phone. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA to access or delete personal data. Since we do not hold your data, most of this is already in your hands; for anything you send us by email, contact us and we will honor your request.

11. Security

Your data is stored in the app’s protected storage on your iPhone and is covered by your device’s own security, such as your passcode and encryption. No method of storage is perfect, but because nothing about you travels to our servers, there is very little for anyone else to reach.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and post the new version here. Meaningful changes will be noted on this page.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@farhanhossain.com.

Your recitation is between you and your Lord. Nothing here records it.
