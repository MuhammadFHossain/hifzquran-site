Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

The short version. Hifz Quran collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of ours. Your progress stays on your phone or iPad and we never see it. The app asks for no permission until you turn on a feature that needs one, and each one is optional: location for prayer times and the qibla, notifications for prayer alerts, and alarms so an adhan comes on time or wakes you. Under Apple's definitions this app is "Data Not Collected."

This policy explains what Hifz Quran ("the app", "we", "us") does with your information. Hifz Quran is made by Farhan Hossain, who is responsible for your information under this policy (the "data controller", in the words of European law). If anything here is unclear, email contact@bluetomatostudio.com.

What changed in this version. The app now runs on iPad, and this policy now also covers the Android app. The two apps work the same way, and where Android is different, this page says so. This version also names the second place the app downloads recordings from, for the duas read aloud. The app still has no microphone access and no speech recognition, still has no accounts, and still keeps everything on your device.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the Hifz Quran app for iPhone and iPad and its widgets, the Hifz Quran app for Android and its widgets, and this website, hifzquran.org. "The app" means all of them unless a line says otherwise.

2. Information we collect

Personal information we collect: none. Hifz Quran has no login, no user accounts, and no server of ours that stores your data. We do not run analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any tracking. The iPhone and iPad app contains no code from other companies (no third-party SDKs). The Android app is built with common free, open-source building blocks for the screen, storage and audio playback, and Google Play's own library for the rating card described in section 6. None of them is analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking.

The app creates and stores the following on your device so it can work:

  • Your reading place. Where you left off, your bookmark, and the passages you have been repeating.
  • Your reading. Which days you read and how many pages, for the count on the Quran tab.
  • Your settings. Small preferences like your chosen print, translation, reciter, repeat counts, tafsir, and theme.
  • Your prayer settings. The place you chose, or the place your location found, how the times are calculated, and which prayers have an alert, an adhan or an alarm turned on. The widgets read the place and the times from this same storage on your device.
  • Recordings you have played or saved, the recitations and the duas read aloud, kept so they play with no internet.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

3. Permissions, all optional

Hifz Quran never asks for a permission when it opens. It asks only when you turn on a feature that needs one. If you say no, the rest of the app works as before.

  • Location, while using the app. Asked only when you tap Use my location. Your position is used on the device to work out the prayer times and the qibla direction. To show the name of your town, the device sends the position to a map service, which turns it into a place name. On iPhone and iPad that is Apple Maps, part of iOS. On Android it is the phone's own place-name service, which on most phones is Google's. Nothing is sent to us, and we never see your location. On Android the app asks only for your approximate location. You can pick a city by name from the built-in list instead, with no permission at all.
  • Notifications. Asked only when you turn on an alert or an adhan for a prayer. These reminders are set on your device itself. Nothing is sent to them from us or from any server. On Android, a notification with the play controls also shows while a recitation is playing, as Android requires for audio that plays with the screen locked.
  • Alarms on iPhone and iPad. Asked only when you turn on Wake me for a prayer, so it can ring like an alarm through the silent switch and a Focus. Set on your device. This needs iOS 26.1 or later.
  • Alarms on Android. When you turn on an alert, an adhan or Wake me, and your phone does not already let the app set exact alarms, the app asks you to allow alarms for Hifz Quran in the phone's settings. Without it, Android may hold an adhan back to save battery. Set on your phone.

Android also gives the app some permissions when it is installed, without asking: to use the internet, to keep playing a recitation with the screen locked, to vibrate, to show a prayer alarm on the screen where Android allows it, and to set the prayer alarms again after the phone restarts. None of these gives the app anything of yours.

The app does not use, and cannot use, the microphone, speech recognition, the camera, photos, contacts or calendars. Your recitation is never heard, recorded, transcribed or stored, by us or by anyone else.

The iPhone and iPad app declares iOS's background audio mode, and the Android app runs a media playback service. That is ordinary playback, so a repeat keeps playing with your screen locked. It carries no access to anything on your device.

4. Where your progress is kept

Everything the app stores stays on your device, in the app's own private storage. Hifz Quran does not copy your progress to your other devices, does not use iCloud, and does not send it anywhere. Your phone and your iPad each keep their own. There is no account attached to it and nothing for us to look at. Deleting the app deletes it. Your device's own backup, if you have turned it on, can include the app's data, as it does for other apps: iCloud or your computer for an iPhone or iPad, and your Google account for an Android phone.

5. Network access

The Quran text, the mushaf pages, the fonts, the translations, the word meanings, the tafsir, the duas, the city list and the adhan recordings are all inside the app and need no network. The app uses the internet for three things:

  • Recitation audio, fetched when you play or save it, from one public Quran audio host, everyayah.com, as one MP3 per ayah for every reciter.
  • Duas read aloud, fetched when you play one, from hisnmuslim.com, which shares the Hisn al-Muslim recordings.
  • Your town's name, only if you tap Use my location, through the map service described above.

The recordings are public files. The requests for them carry no account and none of your progress, and the hosts are not ours. Like any request on the internet, they show the host your internet address. A recording is saved on your device after the first play.

If you tap a link in the app, such as one of the sources in Settings or this policy, the page opens like any web page. Prayer times, the Hijri date and the qibla direction are calculated on the device.

6. No advertising, analytics, or tracking

Hifz Quran contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking software of any kind. We do not build a profile of you and do not track you across other apps or websites. Under Apple's definitions, Hifz Quran is "Data Not Collected," and the App Store privacy label says exactly that.

The store's rating card. Once you have finished three repeats, the app may ask the store to show its own rating card, at most once for each version of the app: Apple's on iPhone and iPad, and Google Play's on Android. The store decides whether to show it. If you rate the app there, the rating goes to the store like any other rating. The app is not told whether you rated it.

7. Purchases

Hifz Quran is free and has no in-app purchases. If we ever added a paid feature, any purchase would be handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, or by Google through Google Play, and we would never see your card or billing details.

8. This website

This site is a simple set of static pages. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies and does not ask you to sign in. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep server logs, including your internet address, to keep the site running and secure. The fonts and pictures are on the site itself, so a visit makes no requests to anyone else.

If you email us

If you email us, we get your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply to you and to fix the problem you tell us about. That is the lawful basis for it: you asked us to help. We keep the message while we are helping you and for a reasonable time after, and we delete it sooner if you ask. We never add you to a mailing list and never pass your email to anyone else. Our email is handled by our email provider, which stores it for us.

9. Children

Hifz Quran is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because the app has no accounts and collects no personal data, there is nothing for a child to submit to us. If a child emails us, we use the message only to reply, and we delete it if a parent asks. If you have a concern, email us and we will help.

10. Your choices and rights

Because your data lives on your device, you are in control of it. You can delete the saved recordings in the app's Settings, and turn any alert, alarm or location use off again. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR in Europe and the UK, and the CCPA in California, give you rights over personal data: to see it, to correct it, to have it deleted, to take a copy, and to object to how it is used. We do not hold any data from the app, so for the app these rights are already in your hands. For an email you sent us, write to us and we will do what you ask within one month. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

We do not sell or share your personal information, as those words are used in California law, and we never have.

11. Security

Your data is stored in the app's protected storage on your device and is covered by your device's own security, such as your passcode and encryption. No method of storage is perfect, but because nothing about you comes to us, there is very little for anyone else to reach.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will change the date at the top and post the new version here. Important changes will be noted at the top of this page. We will never start collecting data from the app without saying so here first.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@bluetomatostudio.com.

Your recitation is between you and your Lord. Nothing here records it.
