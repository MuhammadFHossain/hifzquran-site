The mushaf

The real Madina mushaf.

  • Page 562 here is page 562 in print. Same lines, same fonts.
  • Madina, Classic and Indo-Pak prints, with tajweed colours if you want them.
  • Nineteen translations in seventeen languages. Hold any word for its own meaning.
  • A short tafsir for every ayah, in nine languages, and all of it offline.

Repeat

Play any ayah over and over.

  • Tap an ayah, or pick a range.
  • Set how many times each one plays, and a gap to recite along.
  • Or press Listen to play straight through, ayah by ayah down the page.
  • Thirty-one reciters, every one ayah by ayah. Works offline, and with the screen locked.

Salah

Prayer times for your own city.

  • A countdown to the next prayer, and the whole day on one bar.
  • The qibla, with the distance to Makkah.
  • An adhan or an alarm for each prayer, and widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Dua

Morning and evening duas.

  • The morning and evening sets, duas after the prayer, and durood.
  • Every dua says where it comes from.

Also in the app

The rest of it.

Test yourself

Hide the ayat you are working on, then reveal them one at a time as you recite each from memory.

Pick up where you stopped

The card at the top of the Quran tab takes you straight back to the page you left, and shows how many pages you have read today.

Your days in a row

Read for a few minutes and the day counts. On Friday you get a reminder to read Surah al-Kahf.

The screen stays on

It will not dim or lock while a page is open, so you can read to the end of it without touching the phone.

Find your place

The surah list has all 114 names and shows where each juz starts. On the page itself, the juz is marked in the margin.

Works offline

The mushaf, the Quran text, the translations, the tafsir and the duas are all in the app. Only the recitation audio is downloaded, and only once.

The promise

Free for you.

No account, no ads, no analytics, and no servers of ours. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Location, notifications and alarms are optional, and the app asks only when you turn them on. Read the privacy policy.
