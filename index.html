Repeat

Play any ayah over and over.

  • Tap an ayah. To play more than one, tap the first and the last.
  • Choose how many times each ayah plays, and a pause after it so you can recite.
  • Or press Listen to play from this page to the end of the surah.
  • 31 reciters. It keeps playing when your phone is locked.
  • An ayah you have played once plays again with no internet.

The mushaf

The real Madina mushaf.

  • Page 562 here is page 562 in your printed Quran, line for line.
  • Madina, Classic and Indopak prints. The Madina print can show tajweed colours.
  • The Indopak 15-line pages, checked line by line against the printed book. On iPhone and iPad, most pages show the book's own calligraphy, traced word by word.
  • 19 translations in 17 languages. Press and hold a word to see what it means.
  • A short tafsir for every ayah, in 9 languages. It works with no internet.

Salah

Prayer times for your own city.

  • A countdown to the next prayer.
  • The whole day on one page, with the path of the sun. Swipe to see another day.
  • The qibla, with the distance to Makkah.
  • An adhan or an alarm for each prayer, only if you turn it on.
  • Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Dua

Morning and evening duas.

  • The duas for this time of day come first.
  • The morning and evening sets, duas after the prayer, and durood.
  • Every dua says where it comes from.

iPad

Two pages open, like a printed Quran.

Turn your iPad on its side. The surah list and the meaning sit beside the page. The Salah tab is a large clock you can read from across the room.

More in the app.

Pick up where you stopped

The card at the top of the Quran tab takes you straight back to the page you left, and shows how many pages you have read today.

Your days in a row

The Quran tab shows how many days in a row you have read. On Friday, a note on the Quran tab reminds you to read Surah al-Kahf.

The screen stays on

It will not dim or lock while a page is open, so you can read to the end of it without touching the screen.

Find your place

The Quran tab lists all 114 surahs, or the 30 juz. Tap a juz to open the page where it starts. On the page, a mark in the margin shows where a juz begins.

Works with no internet

The mushaf, the Quran text, the translations, the tafsir and the duas are all in the app. Only the recordings are downloaded, each one the first time you play it.

Light by day, dark after Maghrib

The top of the page takes the colour of the sky at that hour. If you prefer, choose Light or Dark in Settings.

Questions

Before you download.

Is it really free?

Yes, and it always will be. No ads ever, no subscription, nothing to buy, and no account to make.

Is the mushaf the same as my printed one?

Yes. The Madina pages match the printed mushaf line for line. The Indopak 15-line pages were checked line by line against the printed book.

Do I need the internet?

Only the first time you play a recitation. The mushaf, translations, tafsir and duas are inside the app.

Does the app listen to me?

No. The app cannot use the microphone. Nothing you recite is heard or recorded.

Is it on Android?

Coming soon to Google Play. It is the same app, and it will be free.
